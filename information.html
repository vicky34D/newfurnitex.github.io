Behind the Build

Crafting a premium wardrobe: a working guide.

Every perfect wardrobe begins with more than just design — it begins with precision, strength, and the right materials. This is an open record of how we compose a signature Furnitex wardrobe.

1. The Foundation

We start with 19mm Marine-Grade BWP Plywood — the heart of the structure. Built to resist moisture and time, this plywood forms the strong bones of the wardrobe, ensuring it stands tall and steady for decades.

Interior Liners

Inside, we keep it clean and bright using 0.8mm white liner laminates, giving a crisp, spacious, and easy-to-clean look every time you open the doors.

Exterior Face

Outside, it’s all about texture and weight. We apply 1mm textured or glossy laminates, matte walnut oil finishes, or hand-finished veneers that bring the final touch of luxury.

2. Details & Engineering

A beautiful wardrobe must glide effortlessly. Every edge is sealed with 2mm PVC edge banding to prevent peeling and ensure smooth edges. The hardware is equally uncompromising: we use soft-close hinges and full-extension drawer runners sourced exclusively from industry benchmarks like Hettich, Hafele, and Ebco.

💡 Design Tip Conceal LED strip lights with motion sensors behind the shutters. That gentle evening glow instantly elevates the room's atmosphere.

From the first precision-cut panel to the final hand-polished handle, every detail is measured, aligned, and crafted to reward daily attention.
